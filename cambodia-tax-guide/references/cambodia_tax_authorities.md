## 3. Taxpayer Registration

### 3.1 New Registration Process

1. **Determine taxpayer classification**: Small, Medium, or Large (based on annual turnover)
2. **Register with GDT**: Submit registration forms via e-Registration portal
3. **Obtain Tax Identification Number (TIN)**: Assigned upon registration
4. **VAT Registration**: Mandatory if annual turnover exceeds KHR 250 million
5. **Patent Tax**: Register and pay annual patent tax (by March 31)
6. **NSSF Registration**: Register employer and employees for social security

### 3.2 Required Documents

- Business registration certificate (from Ministry of Commerce)
- Articles of incorporation / company charter
- Identity documents of directors/owners
- Lease agreement or property ownership proof for business premises
- Bank account information

### 3.3 Tax Types by Registration

| Tax Type | When to Register |
|----------|-----------------|
| Income Tax (ToI) | At business registration |
| VAT | When turnover approaches KHR 250M threshold |
| Patent Tax | Annual renewal by March 31 |
| Salary Tax (TOS) | When hiring first employee |
| Withholding Tax (WHT) | At business registration (if making qualifying payments) |

## 4. Tax Audit Process

### 4.1 Audit Types

| Audit Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Desk audit | Review of filed returns and supporting documents |
| Field audit | On-site examination of accounting records and operations |
| Comprehensive audit | Full-scope audit covering all tax types and periods |
| Industry-specific audit | Targeted audits for specific industries |

### 4.2 Audit Risk Factors

- Large taxpayers (revenue > KHR 4B / ~USD 1M+): Higher scrutiny
- Transfer pricing: Related party transactions reviewed
- Inconsistent filings: Cross-checking with third-party data
- History of non-compliance or late filings
- Significant changes in revenue or expense ratios
- Industry-specific risk indicators

### 4.3 Audit Process

1. **Notification**: GDT issues audit notification letter
2. **Information request**: Taxpayer provides requested documents
3. **Examination**: GDT reviews documents and conducts interviews
4. **Preliminary findings**: GDT presents initial assessment
5. **Taxpayer response**: Opportunity to explain or provide additional information
6. **Final assessment**: GDT issues final tax assessment
7. **Appeal**: Taxpayer can appeal through GDT appeal process or courts

### 4.4 Proactive Correction

- Companies identifying errors before audit can request penalty reduction
- File overdue returns voluntarily before GDT initiates audit
- Proactive correction is more cost-effective than post-audit penalties
- GDT cross-checks filings against third-party data — gaps increasingly detected

## 5. Penalty Structure

### 5.1 Filing Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Late monthly filing | Up to 10% of tax due + 2% per month interest |
| Non-filing (even if nil tax) | Penalties apply regardless |
| Late annual filing | Up to 10% of tax due + interest |
| Late patent tax renewal | Penalties + potential business license issues |

### 5.2 Payment Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Late payment | 2% per month interest on outstanding balance |
| Underpayment of PTI | Interest charges on shortfall |
| Tax evasion | 10%-40% surcharge on unpaid tax |

### 5.3 Administrative Measures

- **First notification**: GDT sends notification of incorrect tax return
- **Second notification**: Administrative measures for non-compliance (Instruction, Aug 2025)
- **Tax agent license revocation**: GDT can revoke licenses for non-compliant tax agents
- **Business suspension**: In severe cases of non-compliance

- **Website**: https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/

### 6.2 GDT National Tax School

- Offers training courses on:
  - Practical Annual Income Tax Calculation
  - e-Filing for Windows Desktop Application
  - Professional Taxation (certification programs)
  - Tax Obligations Related to VAT
- Training schedules published on GDT website

### 6.3 Tax Agent System

- Licensed tax agents must be registered with GDT
- GDT publishes list of valid tax agent license holders (updated periodically)
- Licenses can be revoked for non-compliance
- Tax agents assist with filing, compliance, and tax planning

### 6.4 Official Exchange Rate

- GDT publishes daily official exchange rate
- Used for tax calculation and filing purposes
- Available at: https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/exchange-rate
- Important for businesses with USD-denominated transactions (tax filed in KHR)
