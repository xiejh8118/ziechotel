# Cambodia Tax System Reference

This document provides comprehensive reference on all Cambodia tax types, rates, thresholds, and filing procedures. Information is current as of 2025-2026 tax year.

## 1. Tax on Income (ToI)

### 1.1 Corporate Income Tax (CIT)

| Taxpayer Type | Rate | Notes |
|---------------|------|-------|
| Standard corporation | 20% | Standard rate for medium and large taxpayers |
| Oil, gas, natural resources | 30% | Including timber, ore, gold, precious stones |
| Insurance - Life | 5% | On gross premium income |
| Insurance - Non-life | 20% | On gross premium income |
| QIP during tax holiday | 0% | 3-9 years depending on sector |
| QIP post-holiday (Option 1) | 25%→50%→75% | Progressive over 6 years after holiday |
| Small taxpayer | 0%-20% | Progressive based on turnover |

**Minimum Tax**: 1% of annual turnover, payable when CIT liability is less than minimum tax. QIPs and companies with proper audited financial statements are exempt.

**Prepayment of Tax on Income (PTI)**: 1% of monthly turnover, creditable against annual CIT. Small taxpayers pay 0.25% quarterly.

**Loss Carryforward**: Tax losses can be carried forward for up to 5 years, subject to conditions (no significant change in business activities).

### 1.2 Tax on Salary (TOS)

#### Resident Taxpayer (Progressive Rates)

| Monthly Salary (KHR) | Monthly Salary (USD approx.) | Rate |
|----------------------|------------------------------|------|
| 0 - 1,500,000 | 0 - $375 | 0% |
| 1,500,001 - 2,000,000 | $375 - $500 | 5% |
| 2,000,001 - 8,500,000 | $500 - $2,125 | 10% |
| 8,500,001 - 12,500,000 | $2,125 - $3,125 | 15% |
| Above 12,500,000 | Above $3,125 | 20% |

**Resident Definition**: Domiciled in Cambodia, principal place of abode in Cambodia, or present in Cambodia for more than 182 days in a tax year (Jan 1 - Dec 31).

**Non-Resident**: Flat rate of 20% on Cambodia-sourced salary income.

**Fringe Benefits Tax (FBT)**: 20% flat rate on:
- Private use of vehicles
- Meals and accommodation
- Utilities (water, electricity, telephone) and domestic servants
- Low-interest loans
- Education unrelated to employee's business activities
- Life and health insurance premiums (except those for all employees)
- Pension contributions exceeding 10% of monthly salary
- Entertainment or recreation expenses

### 1.3 Withholding Tax (WHT)

#### Payments to Resident Taxpayers

| Payment Type | Rate |
|--------------|------|
| Dividends | 0% |
| Technical/Management services | 15% |
| Royalties (intangible assets) | 15% |
| Interest (except banks/financial institutions) | 15% |
| Rent | 10% |
| Term deposit interest | 6% |
| Deposit interest | 4% |

#### Payments to Non-Resident Taxpayers

| Payment Type | Rate |
|--------------|------|
| Interest, royalties, rent, management services | 14% |
| Dividends | 14% |
| Branch profit remittance | 14% |

**DTA Reduced Rates**: Where a DTA applies, reduced WHT rates may be available (see `cambodia_dta_tax_planning.md`).

### 1.4 Capital Gains Tax (CGT)

| Asset Type | Rate | Effective Date |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Real estate | 20% | January 1, 2026 (postponed from 2025) |
| Asset leasing | 20% | September 1, 2025 |
| Investment assets (stocks, bonds) | 20% | TBD |
| Brand names, copyrights | 20% | September 1, 2025 |
| Foreign currency | 20% | September 1, 2025 |

**Note**: Capital Gains Tax implementation for real estate has been postponed multiple times. As of October 2025, the effective date is January 1, 2026. Always verify current status via GDT notifications.

## 2. Value Added Tax (VAT)

| Supply Type | Rate | Notes |
|-------------|------|-------|
| Standard domestic supply | 10% | Most goods and services |
| Export of goods/services | 0% | Zero-rated; input VAT creditable |
| Exempt supplies | N/A | Medical, education, public postal, unprocessed agricultural products |

**VAT Registration Threshold**: Annual turnover exceeding KHR 250 million (~USD 62,500). Mandatory for businesses above this threshold.

**Cross-border digital services**: Since 2024, overseas digital service suppliers must register and remit VAT through a simplified mechanism.

**Input VAT**: Creditable against output VAT, subject to valid invoice requirements and business-relatedness.

## 3. Special Tax (Excise Tax)

### On Goods Production/Distribution

| Product | Rate |
|---------|------|
| Alcoholic beverages | 35% (or specific rates) |
| Tobacco products | 25% |
| Vehicles (by engine size) | 10%-40% |
| Fuel (gasoline, diesel) | Specific rates |
| Telecommunications equipment | 10% |
| Other luxury goods | 10%-30% |

### On Services

| Service Type | Rate |
|--------------|------|
| Telecommunications | 3%-10% |
| Insurance premiums | 3% |
| Entertainment/club services | 10% |

### Sector-Specific Exemptions (2025-2026)

- Domestic cement products: 5% special tax exempted for 2025-2026 (state-funded)
- Electric vehicles (touristic type): Reduced tax rates (notification Aug 2025)

## 4. Other Taxes

### Patent Tax (Annual Business Registration Fee)

| Taxpayer Size | Annual Fee (KHR) | USD Equivalent |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| Small | 400,000 | ~$100 |
| Medium | 1,200,000 - 2,000,000 | ~$300-$500 |
| Large | 3,000,000 - 5,000,000 | ~$750-$1,250 |

**Due**: March 31 each year. Failure to renew results in penalties.

### Public Lighting Tax

- **Rate**: 5% on supply of alcohol and tobacco products at the first stage only

### Accommodation Tax

- **Rate**: 2% on hotel accommodation services

### Signboard Tax

- **Rate**: KHR 100 - 1,500 per dm² based on size and height

### Property Tax (Tax on Immovable Property)

- **Rate**: 0.1% of assessed value of immovable property
- **Threshold**: Properties valued above KHR 100 million (~USD 25,000)
- **Filing**: Annual, by September 30 (extended to December 31 for 2025)

### Tax on Property Rental

- **Rate**: 10% for non-self-assessment taxpayers
- **Rate**: 0.1% for self-assessment taxpayers (immovable property)

### Unused Land Tax

- **Rate**: 2% of market value of unused land
- **Filing**: Annual, by September 30

### Tax on Means of Transportation

- **Rate**: KHR 50,000 - 2,000,000 based on vehicle type, cylinder capacity, weight, or seats
- **Filing**: Annual, by June 30 (with reminder notices in September/October)

### Registration/Stamp Tax

| Transaction Type | Rate |
|-----------------|------|
| Ownership transfer of immovable property | 4% |
| Share contribution in kind | 4% |
| Share transfer / supply contracts (state budget) | 0.1% |
| Company merger and closure | KHR 1,000,000 flat |

## 5. Social Security Contributions (NSSF)

| Contribution | Employer Rate | Employee Rate |
|---------------|---------------|---------------|
| Work injury insurance | 0.8% | - |
| Health insurance | 2.6% | 0.8% |
| Pension fund | 2% | 2% |

**Note**: NSSF contributions are separate from salary tax and must be filed and paid monthly via the GDT system.

## 6. Taxpayer Classification

| Category | Annual Turnover Threshold |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Small | Up to KHR 250 million (~USD 62,500) |
| Medium | KHR 250M - 2,000M (~USD 62,500 - 500,000) |
| Large | Above KHR 2,000 million (~USD 500,000) |

**Self-Assessment Regime**: Medium and large taxpayers are in the self-assessment regime and must maintain proper accounting records, file monthly and annual returns, and undergo potential audits.

## 7. Filing Deadlines Summary

### Monthly (by 20th or 25th of following month)

- VAT return (manual: 20th, e-filing: 25th)
- Prepayment of Tax on Income (20th)
- Withholding Tax (20th)
- Salary Tax (20th)
- NSSF contributions (monthly)

### Quarterly

- PTI for small taxpayers (quarterly based on sector)

### Annual

- Annual Income Tax Return: March 31 (for calendar year taxpayers)
- Patent Tax Renewal: March 31
- Audited Financial Statements: March 31 (medium and large taxpayers)
- Property Tax: September 30
- Unused Land Tax: September 30
- Tax on Means of Transportation: June 30

## 8. Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Late filing | Up to 10% of tax due + 2% per month interest |
| Non-filing (even if nil) | Penalties apply regardless of tax owed |
| Tax evasion | 10%-40% surcharge on unpaid tax |
| Incorrect tax return | Additional tax + interest |
| Failure to comply with 2nd notification | Administrative measures (Instruction Aug 2025) |

**Proactive correction**: Companies that identify and file overdue returns before an audit can request penalty reduction from GDT.
