# Cambodia Tax Incentives & Investment Guide

This document provides detailed information on Cambodia's investment incentive framework, including QIP, SEZ, and sector-specific tax breaks. Information is current as of 2025-2026.

## 1. Qualified Investment Project (QIP)

### 1.1 Overview

A QIP is an investment project registered with the Council for the Development of Cambodia (CDC) or provincial/municipal investment sub-committees. QIP status provides comprehensive tax and duty incentives.

### 1.2 Eligible Sectors (19 categories per Investment Law Article 24)

1. High-tech industries involving innovation or R&D
2. Innovative or highly competitive new industries/manufacturing with high added value
3. Industries supplying regional and global production chains
4. Industries supporting agriculture, tourism, manufacturing, and supply chains
5. Electrical and electronic industries
6. Spare parts, assembly, and installation industries
7. Mechanical and machinery industries
8. Agriculture, agro-industry, agro-processing, and food processing
9. Priority-sector SMEs and SME cluster development, industrial parks, STI parks
10. Tourism and tourism-related activities
11. Special economic zones
12. Digital industries
13. Education, vocational training, and productivity promotion
14. Health
15. Physical infrastructure
16. Logistics
17. Environmental management and protection, biodiversity conservation, circular economy
18. Green energy, climate change adaptation/mitigation technology
19. Other sectors deemed by the Royal Government to have socio-economic potential

### 1.3 Minimum Investment Threshold

- **General**: USD 500,000 in fixed assets (not working capital)
- **Priority sectors or less-developed provinces**: May qualify with lower thresholds
- **Strategic sectors** (healthcare, education in rural areas): Preferential treatment

### 1.4 Basic Incentive Options

QIPs choose ONE of the following two options:

#### Option 1: Tax Holiday

| Period | Incentive |
|--------|-----------|
| Years 1-3/6/9 (by group) | 0% Income Tax exemption |
| Years 1-2 post-holiday | 25% of normal tax due |
| Years 3-4 post-holiday | 50% of normal tax due |
| Years 5-6 post-holiday | 75% of normal tax due |

**Additional benefits during holiday period**:
- Prepayment of Tax on Income (PTI) exemption
- Minimum Tax exemption (requires independent audit report)
- Export Tax exemption

**Investment Activity Categories (Income Tax Exemption Period)**:
- **Group 1 (High technology)**: 9 years exemption
- **Group 2 (Medium technology)**: 6 years exemption
- **Group 3 (Low technology)**: 3 years exemption

#### Option 2: Special Deduction

| Incentive | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Special depreciation | Accelerated depreciation on capital expenditure |
| 200% expense deduction | For up to 9 years (by group) on specific expenses |
| PTI exemption | Same period as Option 1 |
| Minimum Tax exemption | Requires independent audit report |
| Export Tax exemption | Unless other laws provide otherwise |

**Qualifying 200% deduction expenses**:
- Training for Cambodian employees to replace foreign employees
- IT-based accounting system implementation
- Scholarships for Cambodian employees studying abroad
- R&D, foreign expert training on new technologies (Industry 4.0, AI, robotics, big data)

### 1.5 Additional Incentives (Both Options)

| Incentive | Applies To |
|-----------|------------|
| Customs duty exemption on construction materials, equipment | All QIPs |
| Special tax exemption on construction materials, equipment | All QIPs |
| VAT exemption on import of production equipment | All QIPs |
| Customs duty + special tax + VAT exemption on production inputs | Export QIPs and Supporting Industry QIPs only |
| VAT exemption on locally made production inputs | All QIPs |

### 1.6 Expansion of QIP (EQIP)

Existing QIPs can expand and receive additional income tax exemptions:
- **Group 1**: 9 years
- **Group 2**: 6 years
- **Group 3**: 3 years

Exemption starts from first EQIP income generation.

### 1.7 QIP Compliance Requirements

- Annual Certificate of Compliance (CoC) application to CDC
- Annual investment activity report
- Maintain proper accounting records
- File monthly and annual tax returns on time
- Comply with environmental and labor regulations

### 1.8 CDC Application Process

1. **Initial Assessment** (~1-2 weeks): Review business plan against CDC QIP criteria
2. **Application Preparation**: Business plan, financial projections, environmental impact assessment (if required), supporting documents
3. **Submission to CDC**: Electronic and in-person filing
4. **CDC Decision**: Target 28 working days (complex projects may take 2-3 months)
5. **Investment Agreement**: Formal agreement signing with Royal Government
6. **Annual Compliance**: File annual CoC and investment reports

## 2. Special Economic Zones (SEZ)

### 2.1 SEZ Benefits

- Same QIP incentives apply to SEZ-located QIPs
- Additional administrative infrastructure handled by zone developer
- Streamlined customs procedures on-site
- Utilities, security, and infrastructure provided by developer
- One-stop service for customs and administrative matters

### 2.2 Notable SEZs in Cambodia

- Sihanoukville SEZ
- Phnom Penh SEZ
- Bavet SEZ
- Koh Kong SEZ

## 3. Sector-Specific Tax Incentives (2025-2026)

### 3.1 Agriculture Sector (Nov 2025 - Dec 2027)

**Eligible products**: Paddy rice, corn, beans, pepper, cassava, cashew nuts, rubber, Pailin ginger, mangoes, bananas, animal husbandry, aquaculture, domestic palm oil for animal feed

| Incentive | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| VAT on domestic supplies | State-funded (entity does not charge VAT) |
| Input VAT credit | Allowed |
| Minimum Tax | Suspended |
| Dividend Tax | Suspended |
| WHT on services from non-registered persons | Exempted |

**Compliance requirements**:
- Obtain state-paid VAT certificate from GDT
- Provide supplier and customer lists with monthly VAT declarations
- Maintain proper accounting records
- File monthly and annual declarations on time

### 3.2 Water Investment Projects (2025-2030)

**Eligibility**: New or expansion projects by private water operators, recognized by Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation

| Incentive | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Corporate Income Tax | Exempt from date of first qualifying income |
| Prepayment of Tax on Income | Exempt |
| Minimum Tax (1%) | Exempt |
| VAT on domestic purchases | 0% rate during construction phase |

**Goal**: Universal access to clean water by 2030 (Pentagonal Strategy Phase I)

### 3.3 Tourism Sector - Siem Reap

- Tax incentives for businesses in tourism sector in Siem Reap (Prakas, Jan 2026)
- Specific incentives detailed in MEF Prakas

### 3.4 Sihanoukville Investment Program

- Tax incentives under Special Program to Promote Investment in Preah Sihanouk Province (Instruction, Sep 2025)
- Includes investment facilitation and tax relief measures

### 3.5 Cement Industry (2025-2026)

- 5% special tax on domestic cement products exempted for 2 years (2025-2026)
- State-funded: government bears the tax burden
- Entities must use e-Filing system and maintain proper records

### 3.6 Electric Vehicles (EVs)

- Reduced tax rates on means of transportation for EVs (Notification, Aug 2025)
- Applies to: Touristic type EVs, small goods vehicles (pickup), passenger transport vehicles

## 4. 2025-2028 Revenue Mobilization Strategy

The Cambodian government published the **2025-2028 Tax Mobilization Strategy** focusing on:

1. **Tax system modernization**: Digitalization of tax administration
2. **Tax base expansion**: New tax sources including capital gains tax
3. **Tax administration capacity building**: Enhanced audit and compliance
4. **Fair tax environment**: Ensuring equitable treatment across sectors
5. **Digital economy taxation**: Cross-border digital services VAT

## 5. 2026 National Budget Law

Key fiscal highlights:
- **Total budget expenditure**: ~USD 10.2 billion (7.8% increase from 2025)
- **Tax revenue target**: KHR 13.3 trillion (~USD 3.32 billion), ~6.1% of GDP
- **Revenue target adjustment**: 5.4% lower than 2025 target (pragmatic approach)
- **Budget priorities**: Human resource development, digital economy transition, key infrastructure

## 6. Fiscal Policy Trends

- **E-Tax modernization**: Continued expansion of electronic tax systems
- **GDT Chatbot "BEAURAMEI"**: AI-powered tax inquiry assistance (launched Jan 2026)
- **Capital Gains Tax implementation**: Gradual rollout starting 2025-2026
- **Transfer pricing focus**: Increased scrutiny on related-party transactions
- **DTA expansion**: Negotiations with Laos, Philippines, Myanmar, France, Japan, Morocco, UAE
- **Tax Forum**: Annual Cambodia Tax Forum promoting tax compliance
