## 2. Tax Planning Strategies

### 2.1 QIP-Based Tax Planning

**Strategy**: Structure investment to qualify as QIP for maximum tax holiday benefit.

- Choose **Option 1** (tax holiday) for businesses with early profitability
- Choose **Option 2** (200% deduction) for R&D-intensive or capital-heavy businesses
- Time first revenue recognition to start the tax holiday clock optimally
- Apply for EQIP when expanding to extend tax benefits

### 2.2 Holding Company Structuring

**Strategy**: Use DTA-advantaged jurisdictions for holding company structures.

**China-Cambodia DTA example**:
- China holding company with ≥20% ownership → 5% dividend WHT (vs. 14% standard)
- Requires: beneficial ownership, substance, and compliance with treaty conditions
- Tax sparing: Chinese tax incentives recognized by Cambodia

**Singapore-Cambodia DTA example**:
- Singapore holding company → 10% dividend WHT (vs. 14% standard)
- Singapore has no tax on foreign-sourced dividends (if conditions met)
- Requires: substance in Singapore, beneficial ownership

### 2.3 Transfer Pricing Considerations

**Current status**: Cambodia does not yet have dedicated transfer pricing legislation, but:
- Related party transactions are under increased scrutiny
- Market interest rates for related party loans are published annually by GDT
- 2025 market interest rate notifications published (Jan 2026)
- Tax evasion through transfer pricing is subject to 10%-40% surcharge
- DTA implementation management system operational (Instruction, Aug 2025)

**Best practices**:
- Document arm's length pricing for all related party transactions
- Monitor GDT-published market interest rates
- Prepare transfer pricing documentation proactively
- Consider obtaining advance rulings for significant transactions

### 2.4 Related Party Loan Planning

**Strategy**: Optimize related party financing based on GDT-published market rates.

- GDT publishes annual market interest rate notifications for:
  - Loans from related parties (Notification 5097, Feb 2026)
  - General loans (Notification 2158, Jan 2026)
- Interest rates exceeding market rates may be challenged by GDT
- Structure intercompany loans within published market rate bands

### 2.5 Branch vs. Subsidiary Structuring

| Factor | Branch | Subsidiary |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Setup | Extension of foreign company | Separate legal entity |
| CIT | 20% on Cambodia-sourced income | 20% on global income (if resident) |
| Profit repittance | 14% WHT on branch profit remittance | 14% WHT on dividends (DTA may reduce) |
| Minimum Tax | 1% of turnover (unless exempt) | 1% of turnover (unless QIP/audited) |
| QIP eligibility | Not eligible (must be Cambodian entity) | Eligible for QIP incentives |
| Liability | Parent company liable | Limited liability |
| **Recommendation** | For short-term presence | For long-term investment with QIP |

### 2.6 Capital Gains Tax Planning

**Strategy**: Time asset dispositions around CGT implementation dates.

- Real estate CGT: Effective January 1, 2026 (verify with latest GDT notifications)
- Asset leasing, brand names, copyrights, foreign currency: Since September 1, 2025
- Investment assets (stocks, bonds): Date TBD
- Consider pre-CGT transactions for real estate where possible
- Specific exemptions may apply (primary residence, small transactions)

### 2.7 VAT Optimization

**Strategies**:
- Register for VAT if turnover approaches KHR 250M threshold
- Maintain valid VAT invoices for all input VAT claims
- Export-oriented businesses: Zero-rated VAT with input VAT credit
- Agricultural sector: State-funded VAT (2026-2027) — no VAT charged, input VAT creditable
- Water projects: 0% VAT on domestic purchases during construction (2025-2030)

### 2.8 Salary Tax Optimization

**Strategies**:
- Structure compensation to maximize tax-free allowances
- Separate fringe benefits (taxed at 20% flat) from salary (progressive 0%-20%)
- NSSF pension contributions: 2% employer + 2% employee (capped)
- Consider timing of bonus payments across tax periods
- Non-resident employees: 20% flat rate on Cambodia-sourced income only

## 3. Anti-Avoidance Considerations

### 3.1 Tax Evasion Penalties

| Violation | Surcharge |
|-----------|-----------|
| Tax evasion | 10%-40% of unpaid tax |
| Late payment interest | 2% per month |
| Late filing | Up to 10% of tax due |

### 3.2 GDT Audit Risk

- Large taxpayers (revenue > KHR 4B/year): Higher audit scrutiny
- Transfer pricing rules increasingly applied
- GDT cross-checks filings against third-party data
- Proactive correction before audit: Penalty reduction possible
- Administrative measures for non-compliance with second notification (Instruction, Aug 2025)

### 3.3 Substance Requirements

For DTA benefits, ensure:
- Beneficial ownership of income
- Economic substance in the treaty country
- No conduit/treaty shopping structures
- Proper documentation and compliance
